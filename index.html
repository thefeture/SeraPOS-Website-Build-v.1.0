Who is SeraPOS designed for?

The global product is being shaped around electronics, computer, mobile and accessory retailers. Sales, physical stock, customer balances and day-to-day visibility are the focus.

What do the product screens show?

These are SeraPOS interface designs. They illustrate the intended product experience and sample workflows, rather than a live account or a guarantee that every module is released.

How much will it cost?

The starting package is US$10 per month. Our pricing page outlines three packages; higher-tier pricing, exact allowances and billing terms are presented for review before commercial launch.

Can I choose the features I need?

The Plugin Collection is designed around centrally published features that a business can enable when supported. The Invoice Template Library follows the same idea for document layouts.

Can I discuss my business requirements?

Yes. The contact page helps you prepare an inquiry covering your retail setup, location and priorities. Public contact details will be added there before inquiries can be delivered.
